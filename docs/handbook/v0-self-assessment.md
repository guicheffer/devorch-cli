# 🧪 Self-Assessment \- V0

To understand our progress, we will focus on a team-led self-assessment that combines quantitative metrics with qualitative, multi-disciplinary feedback. The goal is to create a clear, holistic picture of how our AI-native approach impacts speed, quality, value, and our collective experience.

This framework is broken into two parts: the metrics we track over time (the "what") and the regular feedback we gather from the team (the "why").

### Part 1: Quantitative Metrics (The "What")

We will track a set of core metrics to measure the objective impact on our discovery and delivery performance.

**Cadence:** Tracked continuously, reviewed bi-weekly.

1. **Discovery:** These two metrics provide a high-level view of our product discovery velocity and effectiveness.  
   * **Idea-to-validated prototype cycle time:** Average time (in hours/days) it takes to go from idea conception → to a validated prototype  
     1. Definition of cycle time: full make cycle.  
   * **% of Prototypes Validated Positively:** Percentage of prototypes that meet success criteria in customer validation tests.  
2. **Delivery**  
   * **Delivery Time:** How long does it take from positive prototype validation to validation of functional availability to customers?  
   * **Rate of Release:** How long to we have between feature releases  
   * **Change Failure Rate:** What percentage of deployments cause a failure in production?  
   * **Time to Restore Service:** How long does it take to recover from a failure in production?

### Part 2: Qualitative Feedback (The "Why")

These multi-discipline surveys assess how teams perceive their product discovery and delivery processes. The results provide leading indication and essential context to quantitative metrics by reflecting team experience and overall workflow health.

#### Cadence: Bi-Weekly Pulse Check

**Core Questions:**

***Team Perception*** 

* **Discovery Velocity:** In the past two weeks working in AI-Native mode, our team has been able to move from idea to validated prototype faster and with less friction than before. (Scale: 1 \= Strongly disagree | 5 \= Strongly agree)  
* **Delivery Speed**: In the past two weeks working in AI-Native mode, we were able to convert an approved PRD into a first working build quickly and without major blockers (Scale: 1 \= Strongly disagree | 5 \= Strongly agree)  
* **Quality & Maintainability:** The code and design assets I worked with (including AI-generated content) were easy to understand, modify, and build upon.(Scale: 1 \= Strongly disagree | 5 \= Strongly agree)  
* **Follow up (why):** Briefly explain the main reason(s) behind your ratings above. (open question)

**Team Experience:** 

* **Team Experience & Flow:** Overall, I felt productive, in flow, and supported by our tools and process while working in this AI-native way.(Scale: 1 \= Strongly disagree | 5 \= Strongly agree)  
* **Positive Experience:** What was one “aha” moment you experienced this/past week working in ai-native PDLC? (open question)  
* **Negative Experience:** Think about your last sprint: what were some specific moments when you felt stuck or slowed down while working in ai-native mode? What was happening, and what did you end up doing to get past it? (open question)

### Connecting the Dots

These surveys provide the qualitative story behind the quantitative data. During the Friday retrospective, teams will review their DORA metrics and KPIs alongside the survey results. This combination allows us to understand not just *what* happened, but *why* it happened, creating a powerful feedback loop for continuous improvement.
