# 🧑‍✈️ Engineering Manager Playbook (EM)

## 🧑‍✈️ Engineering Manager Playbook (EM)

PoC: [Pepijn Senders](mailto:ps@yourcompany.com)

### Objective

Your objective is to maximize team velocity and predictability by managing **context**, not tasks. You will ensure the right AI-native processes are followed, leverage generative AI to maintain strategic oversight, and **lead team [self-assessment]() to continuously improve the overall AI-first workflow**.

### Key tools and services

* **DevOrch:** The primary framework your team uses for consistency.  
  * **Onboarding:** Familiarize yourself with the concept of Profiles. Your role is not to write them, but to empower your team to own their creation and maintenance. Grant your senior engineers access to the [devorch](https://github.com/guicheffer/devorch) repository.  
* **Survey Tools (e.g., Google Forms)**: Standard tools for gathering weekly team feedback.  
* **Onboarding**: Create a simple, anonymous weekly survey for your team.  
* **Generative AI (for oversight):** Your tool for maintaining a clear view of progress.  
  * **Onboarding:** Work with your team to set up simple AI agents or scripts that can parse git, and spec history and pull request summaries to generate automated progress reports.  
* **AI Assistants (Cursor, Claude Code):** The environments where your team executes.  
  * **Onboarding:** No direct action is needed from you, but ensure your team has the necessary licenses and access.

### Expected outcome

The primary outcome of your role is a high-performing team that consistently delivers predictable, high-quality features. You are responsible for two key "handovers":

1. The **environment of clear and unambiguous context** that you create and maintain, which enables your team to produce effective `technical-spec.md` documents.  
2. **Actionable workflow improvements** that you bubble up to the broader organization, based on quantitative and qualitative feedback from your team's self-assessments.

### Example workflows

Here are several ways to execute your role effectively in an AI-native environment.

#### Workflow 1: Managing Context

Your primary function is to ensure the continuous flow of high-quality, unambiguous context to your team.

1. **Act as the Context Guardian:** When an engineer is blocked by an ambiguous spec, your top priority is to get them the clarity they need from Product, UX, or other stakeholders—immediately.  
2. **Review for Clarity, Not Code:** Review key documents like the technical-spec.md not for technical accuracy, but for ambiguity. If a human could misinterpret it, an AI will.  
3. **Improve the Context Loop:** Actively look for ways to improve the quality of context your team receives and produces. Better context means better AI output and less rework.

#### Workflow 2: Championing the Process

Your team's success depends on the consistent application of AI-native workflows.

1. **Coach the "When" and "Why":** Guide your team on when to use Agentic Development for quick, tactical tasks versus Spec-Driven Development for larger, strategic features.  
2. **Empower, Don't Prescribe:** Empower your senior engineers to own and evolve your team's Context Training in DevOrch. Your job is to ensure they have the time and support to maintain these crucial customizations.  
3. **Focus on Outcomes:** Measure what matters: sprint predictability, feature lead time, and team satisfaction.

#### Workflow 3: Leveraging AI for Oversight

Use generative AI to maintain a clear view of progress without adding administrative overhead.

1. **Automate Progress Tracking:** Use AI agents to parse git history, pull requests, and spec documents to generate real-time progress reports.  
2. **Synthesize Information:** Direct AI tools to synthesize updates, identify potential blockers, and summarize the state of a feature. This gives you a high-level strategic overview, allowing you to focus on unblocking the team.

#### Workflow 4: Driving Continuous Improvement

Lead the effort to refine and improve the AI-first workflow based on direct team feedback.

1. **Conduct Pulse Checks:** Use a simple tool like Google Forms to gather anonymous feedback. Questions could include: "On a scale of 1-10, how effective were the AI tools this week?" and "What was the biggest friction point you encountered?"  
2. **Synthesize Feedback with AI:** Export the anonymous survey responses to a spreadsheet. Use an AI agent to process the raw text data, identify recurring themes, and summarize key pain points and suggestions.  
3. **Lead a Self-Assessment Session:** In your team's retro, present the summarized findings and facilitate a discussion focused on actionable improvements.  
4. **Use Feedback to Improve Context**: Analyze the feedback for patterns that indicate a lack of clarity. If engineers consistently report ambiguous requirements, use this data to work with Product and UX to improve the quality and precision of the source documents (`prd.md`, `ux-spec.md`).  
5. **Bubble Up Systemic Issues:** If the feedback points to a systemic issue with the overall AI-first workflow or tools, it is your responsibility to consolidate this feedback and share it with the broader GenAI Driver group.
