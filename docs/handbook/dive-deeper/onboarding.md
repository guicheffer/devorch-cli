# 👋 Onboarding

## 👋 Welcome to AI-Native Product Development

This guide outlines our new, AI-first process for building software. The goal is to integrate AI as a core partner in your workflow, not just as another tool.

This shift is designed to improve team alignment and shipping velocity, allowing you to focus on high-impact problem-solving.

We recommend starting with a hands-on team session, but a self-service path is also available.

### Two Paths to Get Started

We find teams have the most success by starting with a guided session, but a self-service path is also available for those who want to explore at their own pace.

#### 1\. The Team Accelerator Session (Recommended Start)

This is a practical, hands-on workshop to get your team building in the new AI-native workflow. It's the most effective way to align the full squad and set up your new process.

* **What it is:** A typical onboarding involves a mix of hands-on help and guided learning. The time commitment is tailored to your team's needs:  
  * **1-hour** kick-off call with the full team  
  * **5 hours** of intensive mobbing for engineers  
  * **3 hours** of setup and workflow design for PMs  
* **Who it's for:** Entire product squads (PM, UX, Eng) who have opted into the pilot and are ready to commit to the new process.  
* **How to request:** Please reach out to one of the AI-Native champions for your domain:  
  * **Product:** [Ebrahim Kargar](mailto:ekar@yourcompany.com)  
  * **Engineering:** [Pepijn Senders](mailto:ps@yourcompany.com)  
  * **UX:** [Brian Lin](mailto:brian.lin@yourcompany.ca)  
  * You can also ask general questions in the **`#ai-native-pdlc`** channel.

#### 2\. The Self-Service Path

This path is for individuals and teams who want to explore the new process at their own pace.

**Step 1: Understand the "Why".** Start by reading the [**AI-Native Product Development Handbook**](https://docs.google.com/document/d/128Ha0UR0C_bcN50JwGNiV2kJO1YrPQh0UUh0-phsKAE/edit), focusing on the "Core Philosophy" section. This will provide context for this shift.

**Step 2: Learn the Process.** Start by understanding the Think vs Make workflow, then review the playbook for your role.

* [**Workflow Steps**](../workflow-steps/README.md) - Step-by-step guide for both Think and Make cycles. This is your practical reference for how work flows through the process.
* [Product Playbook (⭐☆☆)]()
* [UX Playbook (⭐☆☆)]()
* [Engineering Playbook (⭐⭐☆)]()
* [Engineering Manager Playbook (⭐☆☆)]()

You don't need to master everything; just find one or two new tools or rituals to try.

**Step 3: Try One New Thing.** You don't have to change everything at once. The best way to start is to pick one small thing and try it for a week.

* **Engineers:** Try using **devorch** for your next task.  
* **Product:** Try writing your next spec as a `prd.md` file.  
* **UX:** Try structuring your next Figma file for better AI handoff.  
* **Everyone:** Try the "Demo Daily" ritual.

**Step 4: Ask Questions\!** We are all learning together. Join the **`#ai-native-pdlc`** Slack channel to ask questions, share your learnings, and get support from the community.
