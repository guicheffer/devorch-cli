Welcome, trailblazers\! You are part of a team fundamentally reshaping how we build software. We've redesigned our product development lifecycle (PDLC) around a simple truth: AI enables us to work in a common, natural language, we demo daily, and learn faster.

### The Next Leap in Productivity

To understand why this shift is so important, it’s helpful to look at the evolving role of the engineer. Historically, from the era of the "chief programmer team" to today, the value an engineer can add per minute has constantly increased as tools and processes have improved. The focus was on producing code more efficiently.

We are now at the next major leap. With AI handling much of the heavy lifting in code generation, engineers are able to **"shift left"**: applying their critical problem-solving skills much earlier in the development cycle. This means they can help shape product specifications, ensure technical feasibility from day one, and contribute across the entire value stream. This transforms the engineer's role from a builder who executes a plan to a true partner in defining what gets built.

### The Core Philosophy

AI-Native is about speed, clarity, and alignment. Our new way of working is guided by a few key principles:

* **🤖 Human-in-the-Loop, AI-in-the-Flow:** The AI handles the heavy lifting, but pauses for human intelligence when it encounters ambiguity, empowering us to focus on creative problem-solving.  
* **💬 Natural Language is the Source Code:** We collaborate through clear, concise markdown files that are optimized to produce working software.  
* **✨ E2E Alignment from a Single Source:** We drive end-to-end alignment by using a single, clear specification as the source of truth, ensuring Product, UX, and Engineering are always building the same thing.  
* **🚢 Demo Daily, Ship Weekly:** We demonstrate tangible progress every day, creating a tight feedback loop that culminates in a valuable shipment to production every week.  
* **🤝 We Build on a Supported Stack:** To ensure consistency and velocity, we build upon a common foundation of supported tools and practices. The golden path relies on a standardized toolchain to prevent fragmentation.

### How to Use This Handbook

This handbook details our new AI-Native workflow, which is based on learnings from a **pre-pilot stage** with several early-adopter teams. We are now moving into a formal **pilot phase until the end of Q1 2026,** and we are actively looking for more teams to join us. While the framework is designed for teams, many principles and playbooks can be adopted by **individuals** looking to improve their personal workflows.

* **During the Pilot (Now \- EoQ1):** This is an opt-in phase. We want teams to use these playbooks, "learn first," and help us refine them by encountering and solving issues. Your feedback is critical.  
* **Post-Pilot (After Q1):** Once the pilot concludes, this refined handbook will become the "golden path" for all teams.  
* **Track the Pilot:** Want to add your team or see who else is participating? Check out the[AI-Native Pilot Teams](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1yBWB8BLfjxnHsOuFkst5gdSukbw-lmiGpwKWAYLUf40/edit?gid=0#gid=0).
