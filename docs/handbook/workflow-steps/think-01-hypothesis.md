# 1. Hypothesis

**Cycle:** THINK (Non-Linear)
**Phase:** Discovery

## Purpose

Define a clear, testable hypothesis about a customer problem or opportunity that can be validated through research and prototyping.

## AI-Native Approach

Use AI to rapidly generate and refine hypotheses based on:
- Customer data and feedback
- Market trends
- Competitive analysis
- Business metrics

## Key Activities

- Articulate the problem or opportunity
- Define what success looks like
- Identify assumptions that need validation
- Frame the hypothesis as testable

## Inputs

- Customer insights
- Business objectives
- Market data
- Previous learnings

## Outputs

- **Hypothesis statement**: Clear, testable proposition
- **Success criteria**: How we'll know if it's valid
- **Key assumptions**: What needs to be true
- **Validation approach**: How we'll test it

## Common Pitfalls

- Making the hypothesis too broad or vague
- Skipping the "why now" question
- Not defining clear validation criteria
- Starting with a solution instead of a problem

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Hypothesis is specific and testable
- [ ] Success metrics are clearly defined
- [ ] Key assumptions are documented
- [ ] Validation approach is outlined
- [ ] Hypothesis is customer-focused, not solution-focused
