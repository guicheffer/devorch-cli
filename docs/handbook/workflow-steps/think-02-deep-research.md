# 2. (Deep) Research

**Cycle:** THINK (Non-Linear)
**Phase:** Discovery

## Purpose

Gather and synthesize internal and external context to validate or refine the hypothesis. Deep research de-risks ideas before committing significant resources.

## AI-Native Approach

Leverage AI to:
- Analyze large volumes of customer feedback
- Synthesize market research and competitor data
- Identify patterns and trends
- Extract insights from multiple data sources

## Key Activities

- Review customer data and feedback
- Analyze competitor solutions
- Study market trends
- Gather technical constraints
- Synthesize findings

## Inputs

- Hypothesis statement
- Customer data and feedback
- Market reports
- Competitor information
- Technical documentation

## Outputs

- **Research synthesis**: Key findings and insights
- **Validated/refined hypothesis**: Updated based on learnings
- **Risk assessment**: What could go wrong
- **Opportunity size**: Potential impact

## Common Pitfalls

- Research paralysis (too much research, not enough action)
- Confirmation bias (only looking for data that supports hypothesis)
- Ignoring negative signals
- Not time-boxing research activities

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Multiple data sources have been analyzed
- [ ] Key insights are documented
- [ ] Hypothesis has been validated or refined
- [ ] Risks and opportunities are clearly understood
- [ ] Research findings are synthesized and actionable
