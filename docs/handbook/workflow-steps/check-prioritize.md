# Prioritize

**Cycle:** CHECK (Bridge between THINK and MAKE)
**Phase:** Decision

## Purpose

Stack-rank validated hypotheses into an opportunity backlog. Determine which ideas move from THINK to MAKE based on impact, effort, and strategic alignment.

## AI-Native Approach

Use AI to:
- Analyze multiple prioritization factors
- Calculate effort estimates
- Assess business impact
- Generate prioritization recommendations

## Key Activities

- Assess impact and effort
- Evaluate strategic alignment
- Consider dependencies and risks
- Rank opportunities
- Select what moves to MAKE cycle

## Inputs

- Validated hypotheses from THINK cycle
- Business objectives
- Technical constraints
- Resource availability
- Strategic priorities

## Outputs

- **Opportunity backlog**: Stack-ranked list
- **Prioritization rationale**: Why this order
- **Sprint planning input**: What enters MAKE cycle
- **Deferred items**: What stays in backlog

## Common Pitfalls

- HiPPO (Highest Paid Person's Opinion) prioritization
- Not considering technical dependencies
- Optimizing for easy wins over high impact
- Not revisiting priorities regularly
- Ignoring capacity constraints

## Success Criteria

- [ ] All validated hypotheses are evaluated
- [ ] Prioritization criteria are clear and consistent
- [ ] Stack ranking is documented with rationale
- [ ] Team alignment on what enters MAKE cycle
- [ ] Backlog is living and regularly reviewed
