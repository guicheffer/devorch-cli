# 3. Brainstorm

**Cycle:** THINK (Non-Linear)
**Phase:** Ideation

## Purpose

Generate multiple potential solutions to the validated problem. Explore the solution space broadly before converging on a direction.

## AI-Native Approach

Use AI as a brainstorming partner to:
- Generate diverse solution ideas
- Explore edge cases and alternative approaches
- Challenge assumptions
- Combine ideas in novel ways

## Key Activities

- Generate multiple solution approaches
- Explore technical possibilities
- Consider different user experiences
- Identify quick wins vs. long-term solutions
- Map solution space

## Inputs

- Validated hypothesis
- Research findings
- Technical constraints
- Business objectives

## Outputs

- **Solution options**: Multiple potential approaches
- **Trade-off analysis**: Pros/cons of each option
- **Technical feasibility**: What's possible
- **Preferred direction**: Which option(s) to prototype

## Common Pitfalls

- Jumping to the first "good enough" solution
- Not involving the right people
- Ignoring technical constraints
- Solution bias from past projects

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Multiple solution options have been explored
- [ ] Trade-offs are clearly documented
- [ ] Technical feasibility has been assessed
- [ ] Team alignment on direction to prototype
- [ ] Divergent thinking before convergent decision
